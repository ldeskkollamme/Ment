HOW TO PLAY

  1. Enter Name & Create/Join: Enter your nickname and start a 6-digit room.
  2. 100 Levels with 10 Rounds Each:
    • Each level consists of 10 rounds.
    • Each round has exactly 2 turns (one for each player).
    • Total turns per level: 20 turns.
    • Total turns in the entire game: 2,000 turns.
  3. Number Selection per Level:
    • Level 1: 2 number circles available.
    • Level 2: 3 number circles available.
    • ... up to Level 100 with 101 number circles.
  4. Turn Structure:
    • Turn 1: Player 1 secretly chooses a number → Player 2 guesses. (Correct = Player 2 +1 point)
    • Turn 2: Player 2 secretly chooses a number → Player 1 guesses. (Correct = Player 1 +1 point)
  5. 15-Second Timer: Each turn has a 15-second countdown. Failure to pick/guess awards 0 points.
  6. Anti-Cheat Security: The secret number is sealed and never exposed to the guesser's browser until revealed.
  7. Victory: After all 100 levels (2,000 turns), the player with the highest total score wins!
